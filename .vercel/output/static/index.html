    Why It Was Built (Background & Objectives)

    This Cashier Website was designed to address manual transaction challenges faced by MSMEs and small retail businesses. The goals of this project are to:

    • Speed up the checkout transaction process.
    • Minimize human errors in total bill and change calculations.
    • Automate inventory tracking and daily transaction logging in real-time.

    Key Features

    • Authentication & Role Management: Distinct access rights and login screens for cashiers and administrators.
    • Point of Sale (POS) Interface: Fast product search, quantity input, and automated total and change calculation.
    • Inventory Management (CRUD): Ability to add, edit, and delete product details, prices, and stock counts.
    • Transaction History & Receipt Generation: Transaction record logging with options to print digital or physical receipts.
    • Responsive Layout: Optimized interface for laptops, tablets, and touchscreen POS terminals.

    Tech Stack

    • UI/UX Design: Figma (Wireframing, High-Fidelity Design, Interactive Prototyping)
    • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS / Bootstrap
    • Backend & Database: Node.js / PHP / Laravel & MySQL

    Architectural & Design Decisions

    • Efficiency-First UI: Designed large touch and click targets for numbers and products to speed up cashier navigation without relying heavily on a mouse.
    • Minimal Steps to Checkout: Streamlined the flow from product selection to receipt printing to ensure transactions complete in under 10 seconds.
    • Design System & Reusable Components: Applied Atomic Design principles in Figma for buttons, product cards, and data tables to maintain visual consistency and facilitate developer handoff.
